# Master-Thesis
Optimization of Production Process


Abstract - This thesis delves into the optimization of the ZWOBBEL production process, aiming to enhance overall efficiency through an evaluation of various production steps that directly influence productivity. The study thoroughly investigates the factors contributing to inefficiencies and explores a range of potential solutions to address these challenges. Furthermore, it provides a detailed examination of the design, development, and practical implementation of the identified solutions to ensure meaningful improvements in the production process.

A significant aspect of this research involves the establishment of a quality control checkpoint specifically designed for the inspection of membrane surfaces. By integrating this checkpoint into the production workflow, the study aims to enhance quality assurance measures, thereby minimizing defects and ensuring consistent product standards. The implementation of this quality assessment mechanism plays a crucial role in maintaining high-performance output while reducing waste and rework.
Additionally, this thesis presents an in-depth analysis of the outcomes observed after the implementation of the proposed solutions. By comparing pre- and post-implementation performance metrics, it provides valuable insights into the effectiveness of the optimization strategies employed.
